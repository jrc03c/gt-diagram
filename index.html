GuidedTrack Program Flow

Upload the zip of program source files that GuidedTrack's Package code button produces, and get a Mermaid flowchart of which programs call which. Only *program: and *switch: lines are read; nothing else in your programs is represented. The zip is processed entirely in your browser and never leaves your computer.
